## Highlights

Lightshot 0.4.0 looks right in both **Light and Dark mode**. Before, the recording toolbar and the Studio editor were always dark, even when the rest of your Mac was light.

**Light and Dark everywhere**
- The recording toolbar, the microphone and camera picker, the recording controls and the Studio editor now follow your Mac's appearance: light in Light Mode and dark in Dark Mode. Dark Mode looks exactly as it did before.
- Switching appearance, including Auto at sunset, updates every open window straight away. The Studio editor keeps your place, your selection and your undo history.
- New setting: **Settings → General → Appearance**, with **Match System** (the default), **Light** or **Dark**. Pick Dark to keep the old dark recording toolbar and Studio editor whatever your Mac is set to.

**Your recordings and screenshots don't change**
- Screenshots, annotations and exported videos look identical whichever appearance you use.
- Anything drawn over your screen while you capture keeps its colours in both modes, so you can see it against whatever is on screen: the dimming, the recording border, the countdown and the camera bubble.

**Fixes**
- The menu-bar timer while recording was drawn black, so you couldn't read it on a dark menu bar. It now shows a red stop icon and a time that matches the menu bar.
- Colour swatches in the screenshot editor now have an edge that shows in both modes, so black and white swatches are easy to see.

Redaction reminder: only **blackout** erases pixels. Blur and pixelate only obscure, so don't rely on them to hide secrets.
