## Highlights

Lightshot 0.5.0 makes the Studio video editor easier to cut and restyle, and adds **Auto Redact** to the screenshot editor.

**Trim and speed, the same way as zooms**
- **Trim** is now a pill on its own track, just like a zoom or a text. Put the playhead where you want it and press **T** (or the scissors button), then drag or resize the pill. The trimmed part is skipped when you play the video and left out of the export.
- The timeline always shows your whole recording. Trimmed parts are dimmed on the filmstrip, so you can see what you cut and bring it back.
- **Speed Up** and **Slow Down** add a pill on the Speed track. That stretch plays at 2× or 0.5×; right-click the pill to pick anything from 0.25× to 4×. Audio keeps its pitch.
- Split and dragging clip edges are gone; trim pills replace them. Cutting words in the transcript and **Remove Silences** now add trim pills too.
- Projects made with an earlier version open with their cuts turned into trim pills and their sped-up clips turned into speed pills. Once you edit one, older versions of Lightshot can no longer open it.

**Cursor styles**
- The Cursor panel has a **Cursor Style** grid with ten looks: macOS, White, Pink, Mint, Violet, Blue, Neon, Outline, Bold and Yellow. The style you pick shows in the preview and in the export.
- Click effects are now **yellow** by default, the same colour as the recorder's click highlight.

**Cleaner defaults**
- New studio projects start with **no padding and no rounded corners**, so the recording fills the frame until you add them. Projects you already have keep their settings.

**Auto Redact in the screenshot editor**
- The redact tool has an **Auto Redact** button (⇧⌘R). It finds sensitive text on your Mac and covers it in one step: secrets and API keys, payment cards, IBANs, emails, phone numbers, IP addresses, links, addresses, faces and codes. Nothing leaves your Mac.
- It uses the redaction style you have selected. The whole batch is one undo step, and each redaction can be moved, resized or deleted afterwards.
- The chevron next to the button lets you choose which kinds of data to look for.
- Recognition can miss things, so check the result before you share it.

**Also new**
- While you record, a **red pulsing border** shows exactly what is being recorded. It stops pulsing while you are paused, and it never appears in the video.
- Text in the screenshot editor now starts at **20 px** instead of 32 px, and new step markers are smaller to match.

Redaction reminder: only **blackout** erases pixels. Blur and pixelate only obscure, so don't rely on them to hide secrets.
