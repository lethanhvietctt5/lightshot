## Highlights

Lightshot 0.8.0 lets you fine-tune a Capture Area selection before the screenshot is taken.

**Adjust the area before capturing**
- A new toggle, **Adjust the area before capturing**, is in **Settings → Screenshots → Capture**. It's off by default, so Capture Area still captures the moment you release the mouse unless you turn it on.
- With it on, releasing the drag leaves the area on screen, with handles and its size in pixels. You can:
  - drag inside it to move it;
  - drag a handle to resize it;
  - nudge it with the arrow keys (⇧ resizes);
  - drag anywhere outside it to draw a different area.

  A stray click doesn't throw away your adjustments.
- Click **Capture** next to the area, or press Return, to take the screenshot. Esc cancels. The screen stays frozen while you adjust, and the screenshot opens in the editor or as a Quick Access card, as before.
- It works across several displays, with Repeat Last Capture, and with the self-timer, where the countdown starts when you click Capture. OCR Text still copies the moment you release.

Redaction reminder: only **blackout** erases pixels. Blur and pixelate only obscure, so don't rely on them to hide secrets.
