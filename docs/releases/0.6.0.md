## Highlights

Lightshot 0.6.0 freezes your screen while you capture, and adds **OCR Text** for copying text you can't select.

**Freeze Screen**
- When you start **Capture Area**, **Capture Window** or **OCR Text**, the whole screen freezes at that moment. Videos stop on the frame you saw, and open menus, tooltips and notifications stay put while you select.
- What you capture is exactly what you saw when you pressed the shortcut: an area is cut from the frozen screen, pixel for pixel.
- **Capture Window** gives you the window as it looked at that moment, on its own and with nothing overlapping it, even if it changes or closes while you pick it.
- The selection overlay and the window picker now work on **every display**, not only the main one. Each display is captured at its own resolution.
- Freezing is always on. With a self-timer set, captures still wait and then take the live screen, so the timer keeps working as before.

**OCR Text**
- A new **OCR Text** item sits under Record Screen in the menu bar. Drag over any part of the screen and the text in it is copied to your clipboard as plain text, with lines kept in reading order.
- It works on text you can't normally select: screenshots, videos, dialogs, images in PDFs. Recognition runs on your Mac; nothing is uploaded.
- A small notice shows the first line of what was copied, or "No text found" (your clipboard is left alone).
- You can give it a shortcut in Settings. It has none by default.
- Recognition can misread similar characters, such as 0 and O, so check important text before you use it.

Redaction reminder: only **blackout** erases pixels. Blur and pixelate only obscure, so don't rely on them to hide secrets.
