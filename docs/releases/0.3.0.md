## Highlights

Lightshot 0.3.0 turns the video editor into a **Studio editor**, in the spirit of CleanShot X's Studio Mode and OpenScreen. Every recording is now fully editable after the take. Still local-only: no cloud, no accounts, no network. Even captions are transcribed on your Mac.

**Every recording is editable**
- Recordings now capture a clean screen, with your pointer, clicks and keystrokes as data and your camera as its own track. The cursor, zooms, camera and keystrokes can all be changed after the take.
- With **After recording → Open editor** (or **Record in Studio Mode**) the take opens straight in the Studio editor. Otherwise it's rendered with the look you recorded with, for a quick share, and **Open Video Editor** later brings back the editable version.
- Recent studio projects are in the menu bar under **Studio Projects**.

**Zoom and cursor**
- **Auto Zoom** places smooth zooms where you clicked; add zooms by hand and drag or resize them on the timeline. Each zoom follows the cursor or looks at a point you click, with adjustable easing and motion blur.
- The cursor is redrawn from data: size, smoothing, motion blur, hide when idle, ripple or pulse click effects, or hidden entirely.

**Canvas**
- Backgrounds: colour, gradient, generated wallpapers or your own image, with blur. Add padding, rounded corners and a shadow.
- Aspect ratios for every platform: Auto, 16:9, 4:3, 1:1, 4:5 and 9:16.

**Timeline**
- Split (S), delete and trim clips; set a speed per clip (0.25×–4×, pitch kept).
- Zoom and text lanes, snapping to the playhead and nearby edges, an audio waveform, and undo / redo for everything. Projects save automatically.

**Captions and text**
- **Transcribe Narration** on this Mac, then burn in styled captions.
- Cut speech by selecting words in the transcript, or **Remove Silences** in one click.
- Title cards and callouts on their own track; drag them into place on the preview.

**Camera and keystrokes after the take**
- Move (drag on the preview), resize, reshape, mirror or hide the camera; show, hide or restyle keystrokes.

**Export**
- MP4 or GIF, H.264 or HEVC, source size to 4K, 24 / 30 / 60 fps and a quality slider, with a size estimate. Exports land in your recordings folder and History.

**Recording**
- Picking a window now records its **area**, so apps you open over it are in the take.
- Switching the microphone or camera on lets you **choose the device** right away; the ▾ on the button reopens the list.
- A smaller default recording area (a centred 16:9 region), and a cleaner controls pill with no grey box around it.

Redaction reminder: only **blackout** erases pixels. Blur and pixelate only obscure — don't rely on them to hide secrets.
