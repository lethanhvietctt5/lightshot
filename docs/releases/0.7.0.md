## Highlights

Lightshot 0.7.0 keeps itself up to date, and adds Quick Access cards, QR code reading, translation, and clutter-free captures.

**Updates, in the app**
- Lightshot can now check for new versions and install them itself. On its second launch it asks whether to check automatically (about once a day); nothing goes online until you say yes.
- **Check for Updates…** is in the menu-bar menu and in Settings → About. **Settings → General → Updates** has the automatic-check and automatic-install toggles and **Check Now**.
- An update is verified against the maintainer's signature, replaces the app where it is and relaunches it. Your permissions carry over, and there's no "Apple could not verify" warning after an in-app update.
- If a daily check finds an update while you're working, a dot appears on the menu-bar icon instead of a window. The relaunch waits until a recording or GIF conversion has finished.
- The update check is Lightshot's only network access. It fetches the release list from GitHub; your screenshots and recordings never leave your Mac.
- **Coming from 0.6.0 or earlier?** Those versions can't update themselves, so install this one by hand once. After that, updates come to you.

**Quick Access cards**
- With After Capture set to **Show Quick Access Overlay**, each screenshot waits as a card in the bottom-left (or bottom-right) corner. Take several and they stack up.
- Hover a card to **Copy**, **Save**, **Annotate**, **Pin** or **Close** it, double-click to annotate, or drag it straight into another app. Right-click for Save As… and Close All.
- Cards can close themselves after 10 seconds, 30 seconds or a minute. Set this in **Settings → Screenshots → Quick Access**.
- Fullscreen screenshots now follow the After Capture setting too.

**OCR Text extras**
- A **QR code or barcode** in the selected area is decoded, and its content is copied.
- The "Text copied" notice has a **Translate** button that uses macOS's built-in translation (macOS 14.4 and later).
- **Keep line breaks** (Settings → Screenshots → OCR Text) chooses between one line per row and a single paragraph.

**Less clutter in captures**
- **Hide desktop icons in screenshots and recordings** (Settings → General) shows your wallpaper where the icons are. While recording, the icons are also hidden on screen until the take ends.
- **Hide notifications** (Settings → Screen Recording) keeps notification banners out of recordings.

Redaction reminder: only **blackout** erases pixels. Blur and pixelate only obscure, so don't rely on them to hide secrets.
