## Highlights

Lightshot 0.7.1 makes Studio exports much smaller and faster, and fixes two annoyances.

**Smaller, faster Studio exports**
- The same look now takes far fewer bytes. A 94-second Retina edit that exported at 95 MB in 0.7.0 now comes out at 34 MB (HEVC). Mostly static screen recordings shrink even more.
- HEVC exports finish in about half the time. GIF exports are nearly five times faster: 12 s instead of 57 s for the same edit.
- A new edit now exports at the frame rate you recorded at, 30 fps by default, instead of always 60. That halves the export time for those takes. Projects you've already opened keep their setting, and **Output → Frame Rate** still offers 24, 30 and 60.
- The estimated size now reads like "≤ 49 MB". It's the most the file will take; screen recordings usually come in well under it.
- Recordings you share without opening the Studio are encoded the same way, so they get smaller and faster too.

**Fixes**
- On the Studio timeline, short trim, speed, zoom and text pills can be resized by their edges again. Before, only longer pills could.
- **Capture Fullscreen** is no longer greyed out in the menu when more than one display is connected.

Redaction reminder: only **blackout** erases pixels. Blur and pixelate only obscure, so don't rely on them to hide secrets.
