## Highlights

Lightshot 0.2.0 adds **screen recording** and gives the editor, the video editor and Settings a CleanShot X-style redesign. Still local-only: no cloud, no accounts, no network.

**Record your screen**
- **Record Screen** from the menu bar: drag an area (or pick a window or a whole display), adjust it with handles, lock an aspect ratio or type an exact size, then record a **video** (↩) or a **GIF** (⌥↩).
- A compact recorder toolbar with toggles for **microphone**, **computer audio**, **camera**, **click highlighting** and **keystrokes**, and a warning badge when a toggle still needs a permission.
- While recording: pause / resume, stop, restart and discard from a small controls pill or the menu-bar timer; optional countdown, sounds and dimming outside the recorded area; crash recovery for long takes.
- Your camera as a draggable bubble (size, shape, mirror; click it for fullscreen), highlighted clicks, and keystrokes shown as one pill per burst of typing — never while a password field has focus.
- **Record in Studio Mode**: a video that opens straight in the video editor.

**After recording**
- A post-recording overlay to copy, save, rename, drag out, Quick Look or delete the take.
- A studio-style **video editor**: filmstrip timeline with trim handles, size, quality and audio (mute, volume, mono, remove) with a live output summary and estimated file size; export as a new file or replace the original, with Revert.
- GIF conversion with progress, cancellable back to the video.
- Recordings live in Capture History next to your screenshots, with thumbnails and durations.

**Annotate**
- One compact toolbar row beside the traffic lights; the options change with the tool or the selected mark.
- New **Focus** tool: dim everything outside one or more areas.
- Text labels in a padded box that wraps; drag the side handles to set the width, the corner to scale the text; click a label again to edit it.
- A CleanShot-style crop with a thirds grid, corner brackets and resize / move cursors.
- Any drawing tool can pick up and move an existing mark; new marks are no longer auto-selected.

**Settings & more**
- A redesigned Settings window with a sidebar (General, Shortcuts, Screenshots, Screen Recording, Advanced, About) and an **After Capture** table for screenshots and recordings.
- Permissions for microphone, camera and Input Monitoring are asked for only when you first turn the feature on.
- Capture History opens larger and closes itself when you open an item.

Redaction reminder: only **blackout** erases pixels. Blur and pixelate only obscure — don't rely on them to hide secrets.
